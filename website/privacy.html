Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This policy explains how SmeOps collects, uses, stores and shares information when a business uses the SmeOps workspace or a customer communicates with a participating business through WhatsApp. SmeOps is being developed for appointment-based small businesses, initially in Nigeria.

1. Who this policy applies to

“SmeOps”, “we” or “our” refers to the service operator of the SmeOps application. This policy covers business owners and authorised team members who use the workspace, and customers whose information is processed as part of a participating business's customer conversations and appointments.

2. Information we process

  • Business account information: business name, tenant/workspace details, owner or manager email address, authentication data, services, prices and opening hours.
  • Appointment information: customer name, contact details, selected service, requested date/time, appointment status and related operational records.
  • WhatsApp information: WhatsApp business identifiers, customer phone number, message content, message timestamps, delivery status, opt-out state and conversation history.
  • Technical and security information: authentication/session records, rate-limiting data, webhook event identifiers, audit records and service/worker health information.
  • Meta onboarding information: authorisation and WhatsApp Business Account/phone-number identifiers needed to connect a business number. Business access tokens are stored encrypted.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and secure business workspaces; configure services and opening hours; receive and process WhatsApp messages; check appointment availability; create and manage booking records; display conversations and appointments to authorised business users; deliver messages and reconcile delivery status; handle opt-outs and human escalation; prevent abuse; troubleshoot reliability and maintain the service.

4. AI-assisted message classification

A business may enable optional AI-assisted intent classification. When enabled, relevant message text may be sent to the configured AI service provider for classification. This classification does not have authority to access the SmeOps database, set prices, select appointment availability or create bookings. Booking and availability decisions are handled by application rules, and an appointment requires explicit customer confirmation.

5. How information is shared

  • The business you contact: messages and booking details are made available to the business operating the WhatsApp number so it can respond and provide its services.
  • Meta/WhatsApp: messages and required account information are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to provide WhatsApp messaging and account connection features. Meta processes information under its own terms and privacy policies.
  • Infrastructure providers: hosting, database and operational providers process information on our behalf to run the application.
  • Optional AI provider: message text is shared only when the optional intent-classification integration is enabled.
  • Legal and safety reasons: information may be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or others.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use customer WhatsApp message content to make advertising profiles.

6. Storage and security

SmeOps uses access controls and technical safeguards including password hashing, protected owner sessions, tenant-scoped records, encrypted storage for WhatsApp access tokens and selected customer/message data, webhook signature verification and durable processing records. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Businesses are responsible for controlling access to their workspace and using their WhatsApp account in accordance with applicable terms.

7. Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain business records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect the platform. Specific retention and deletion periods may depend on the business's configuration and applicable law. A customer should contact the business they messaged to ask about its appointment/customer records. A business owner may contact SmeOps about account-level data requests.

8. Choices and rights

Customers can use WhatsApp's available controls and follow the business's opt-out instructions to stop further messaging. The application records opt-out state and prevents messaging where the opt-out rules apply. Depending on applicable law, individuals may have rights to request access, correction or deletion of personal information. Requests should identify the relevant business and information without sending passwords or security credentials.

9. International processing

Our hosting and service providers may process information in countries other than the country where a business or customer is located. Where required, appropriate safeguards should be used for cross-border processing.

10. Children

SmeOps is a business operations service and is not designed for children to create accounts. Businesses are responsible for ensuring that their use of customer information is lawful and appropriate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date at the top indicates when this version took effect. Material changes should be communicated through the website or service.

12. Data deletion requests

Individuals can request deletion of eligible personal information using the dedicated User Data Deletion page. The page explains how to submit and verify a request, including requests involving SmeOps accounts and customer appointment information.

13. Contact and privacy requests

For questions about this policy or requests concerning information processed by SmeOps, email techsolutehub@gmail.com. For appointment details held by a salon or other business, contact that business directly in the first instance.

SmeOps support and privacy requests

Send an email with “Privacy request” in the subject. For deletion requests, use “User data deletion request”. Do not include passwords, access tokens or full payment credentials.
