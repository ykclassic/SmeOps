User Data Deletion

Effective date: 6 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of personal information associated with SmeOps. You can make a request whether you are a business account holder or a customer who has communicated with a business using WhatsApp.

1. Request deletion of a SmeOps account or workspace data

  1. Email techsolutehub@gmail.com with the subject “User data deletion request”.
  2. In your initial email, identify the type of account or information involved at a high level. Do not include passwords, access tokens, full payment details, customer message contents or other sensitive personal information.
  3. We will respond through email and, if necessary, establish a private verification process to confirm that you are the account holder or are authorised to make the request.
  4. Tell us whether you are requesting deletion of a SmeOps owner account, workspace information, or another category of information. Do not send more information than is needed to identify the request.
  5. After verification, SmeOps will assess the request, explain any information that must be retained by law or for legitimate security, dispute-resolution or record-keeping purposes, and arrange deletion or de-identification of eligible information.

2. Customer WhatsApp conversations and appointment records

If you are a customer of a salon or another business using SmeOps, contact that business directly and ask it to delete the customer information and appointment records it controls. The business may be the appropriate party to handle the request because it collected the information to provide its services. If you believe SmeOps separately holds information about you, you may also submit a request using the email address above.

3. WhatsApp messages and Meta account data

Deleting information from SmeOps does not itself delete messages or information retained by WhatsApp/Meta, or copies held on your device or by the business you contacted. Review the applicable WhatsApp and Meta privacy controls and submit any separate request to those services where necessary. Disconnecting a WhatsApp Business account from SmeOps also does not automatically delete information already retained by Meta or the business.

4. Verification and processing

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity or authority before acting. We will not ask you to disclose your password or access token. We may need to retain limited information where required by applicable law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, prevent fraud or maintain security. Where retention is required, we will restrict use to the relevant purpose and retain it only as permitted or required.

We will communicate the outcome through the private contact channel established for the request. Processing time depends on the scope of the request, verification and any applicable legal requirements. We do not publish a fixed completion period on this page.

5. Contact and related policy

For details about the categories of information SmeOps processes, why it is processed, sharing and retention, read the SmeOps Privacy Policy. For general support, visit the Contact page.

Submit a data deletion request

Email the SmeOps privacy team. Please keep passwords, access tokens and unnecessary sensitive personal information out of your initial request.
